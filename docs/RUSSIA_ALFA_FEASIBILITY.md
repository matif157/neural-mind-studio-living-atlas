# Russia scholarship / ALFA feasibility — Pakistani student, PKR 1.5m

Research date: **25 November 2026**. Legal routes only: no undeclared work, false documents, or visa misuse.

## 1. Verdict

**PKR 1.5m ≈ USD 5,428 ≈ RUB 446,000 ≈ MYR 22,311.**

- **Self-funded bachelor’s in Russia is not feasible on this budget.** Even a cheap regional public CS/IT route is about **RUB 1.72m–2.13m / PKR 5.77m–7.15m** for one prep year + four years of dorm-based living. ITMO is **PKR 8.88m–11.57m**; premium Innopolis/HSE is **PKR 13.59m–19.52m**.
- **Feasible only with a full quota/grant.** With free tuition + free prep, the remaining living gap is about **PKR 2.06m–2.87m over four years** after the small quota stipend. If the separate Interobrazovanie grant is also obtained, its **RUB 27,093/month** plus travel/insurance/accommodation support can close that gap — but it is limited (~2,000 awards in 2026) and conditional on good/excellent grades.
- **“Go to Russia on a language course, then win a scholarship locally” is not a reliable plan.** The main Government Quota selects candidates in the applicant’s home country first. Open Doors is online and can technically be entered from anywhere, but registration for the 2026 cycle closed **1 Nov 2026** and eligibility is strict. A language course helps admission/Russian level; it does not itself create a scholarship right.

## 3. All-in Russia cost without scholarship

Assumptions: 4-year bachelor + one prep year where Russian is needed; dorm-based living **RUB 15,000–20,000/month** (derived from ITMO’s published dorm RUB 1,400–5,500, food from RUB 10,000, transport RUB 802–1,599, mobile from RUB 500). Excludes international flights, visa/medical/legalization fees, and emergencies.

| Scenario | Tuition evidence | Total RUB | Total PKR | Gap vs PKR 1.5m | Budget covers |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cheap regional public CS/IT | SUSU RUB 219k–250k/year | 1.72m–2.13m | 5.77m–7.15m | 4.27m–5.65m | 21–26% |
| ITMO / strong mid-range CS | ITMO RUB 450k–579k/year | 2.64m–3.44m | 8.88m–11.57m | 7.38m–10.07m | 13–17% |
| Premium Innopolis/HSE | RUB 800k–1.17m/year | 4.04m–5.81m | 13.59m–19.52m | 12.09m–18.02m | 8–11% |
| **Prep year only, self-funded** | Prep RUB 120k–165k + living | 0.30m–0.41m | 1.01m–1.36m | 0–0.14m | 100% at low end |

Prep-year official examples: UTMN **RUB 120k/135k**, CSPU **RUB 130k**, SUSU **RUB 155k–165k**, Moscow Poly from **RUB 160k**. The budget can survive a frugal prep year, but leaves essentially nothing for the degree.

## 5. ALFA Malaysia comparison

- Budget = **MYR 22,311**.
- Published CS fee mirror: **RM 48,000** total tuition + **RM 10,500** initial international fees = **RM 58,500 ≈ PKR 3.93m** — already **PKR 2.43m** above budget before living costs. ALFA’s official international page says year-1 tuition is payable before arrival.
- User’s all-in estimate **RM 110,000–135,000 ≈ PKR 7.40m–9.08m**: gap **PKR 5.90m–7.58m**; budget covers only **16–20%**.
- ALFA scholarships up to 100% are advertised for **Central Asian** students; Pakistan is not listed. Do not assume a Pakistan scholarship.

## 6. Uncertainty / conflicts — must verify before paying

1. **Pakistan nomination body.** One source says the Russian House in **Karachi** is the only authorised nominating body; another says the Russian House in **Islamabad** has nomination rights. The official Russian House page was anti-bot blocked during this research. Confirm with both/embassy before relying on a deadline.
2. **Quota deadline.** A Pakistan scholarship guide says **15 Jan 2027** for non-CIS countries; HSE says applications open **15 Nov 2026** and to check the local Rossotrudnichestvo deadline; a RCSC Pakistan snippet reportedly says the period was postponed until December with date TBD; Innopolis still shows a stale **15 Jan 2026**. MISIS says 2027/28 regulations will be published by **20 Jan 2027**. Treat all dates as indicative until confirmed locally.
3. **Scholarship money.** Open Doors FAQ describes the quota as tuition + monthly stipend, with travel/accommodation/food/insurance as student costs. The separate Interobrazovanie grant adds the larger monthly payment and travel/insurance/accommodation support. These are different benefits and are often conflated in news articles.
4. **Costs vary by city and university.** Figures above use official 2026/27 pages where available; Moscow/St Petersburg and private-style universities are much more expensive than regional publics.
5. **Open Doors next cycle.** Not published as of 25 Nov 2026. The 2026 registration window is closed.

## 7. Legal work note

Full-time students at state-accredited institutions may work without a work permit during study, but must give the employer a university certificate updated each semester; the employer must notify authorities within 3 days. A temporary residence permit for study also allows work without a separate permit. **Do not build the funding plan on casual work** — Russian-language job hunting is slow and study remains the priority. Illegal work was excluded from this analysis.
