# Post-bachelor legal work: Russia vs Malaysia (Pakistani CS/IT graduate)

Research date: **25 November 2026**. Scope: **legal work only** — no undeclared work, false documents, visa misuse, or “work on a tourist/student visa” shortcuts. This is research, not legal advice; confirm with the employer, JIM/EMGS/MIDA or Russian immigration counsel before relying on it.

## 1. Verdict

**Short answer: Malaysia is the lower-risk country, but an ALFA bachelor graduate from Pakistan has no easy automatic post-study work route. Russia has a higher legal salary ceiling only through employer sponsorship/HQS or an accredited IT employer, but junior pay is far below the HQS threshold and sanctions/banking risk is material.**

- **Malaysia / ALFA:** The degree is listed by MQA, but **Pakistan is not on the published Graduate Pass nationality list**, so the 12-month Graduate Pass is not available to a Pakistani graduate under the current list. Full-time work needs an employer-sponsored Employment Pass (EP). From **1 Jun 2026** the general EP Cat III floor is **RM5,000/month**, while common junior developer pay is **RM3,000–5,500/month**. Only a narrower MIDA “EP Foreign Graduate” route has a **RM4,000** floor, and it is limited to five manufacturing sectors plus **CGPA ≥3.50** (or Master’s/PhD). Net: **possible, but not automatic**.
- **Russia:** Legal work after graduation generally requires an employment contract plus work permit and work visa, or the HQS route, or employment by a state-accredited IT organisation that can use the no-work-permit route. Junior IT pay is around **RUB 50,000–120,000/month** depending on city/role, while the 2026 HQS threshold is about **RUB 250,000/month**. Net: **possible, but junior salaries do not support HQS; success depends on Russian language, internship conversion, and finding a sponsor/accredited IT employer**.
- **Risk:** Malaysia has no comparable Russia-sanctions barrier. Russia carries additional employer-compliance, payment, client, and reputational risk from U.S. sanctions rules, even where a Pakistani national is not directly designated.

**Practical conclusion:** For a Pakistani CS bachelor, Malaysia is the safer base **only if** you can secure (a) an EP offer at/above **RM5,000 basic monthly salary**, or (b) a qualifying MIDA graduate-EP role in one of the five allowed sectors with **CGPA ≥3.50**. Russia is a higher-upside but higher-friction path: target **accredited IT employers + Russian B1+ + internship-to-offer**, and do not plan on HQS at junior salary.

## 3. Russia

### 3.1 Legal routes after bachelor’s

| Route | What it gives | Main constraints for a Pakistani CS/IT bachelor |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard work permit + work visa** | Job with an employer sponsor; permit tied to employer and position. | Employer sponsorship required. 2026 quota is **278,940 specialists** under Decree No. **1920** dated **28.11.2025**. Non-quota categories are under Ministry of Labour Order No. **475n** dated **23.09.2013**. Russian-language/history/law certificate issue is conflicting — see §6. |
| **HQS — Highly Qualified Specialist** | No quota; decision in about **14 working days**; status up to **3 years**; sources indicate no Russian-language certificate for HQS. | 2026 salary threshold **RUB 750,000/quarter ≈ RUB 250,000/month**. From **1 Mar 2027**, reported threshold **RUB 717,000/month**. Junior IT pay is far below this. Threshold is likely gross salary; confirm. |
| **State-accredited IT organisation route** | No separate work permit if the employer is a state-accredited IT company; still needs work visa invitation, contract, and employer notifications to MVD/Social Fund. | Narrower employer set. This is the most relevant “no work permit” IT route, but it is **not** “no visa” and not a freelance workaround. |
| **Student work during study** | Full-time students at state-accredited institutions can work without a work permit during study with a university certificate updated each semester; employer notifies within 3 days. | Ends when student status ends. A temporary residence permit for study also allows work without a separate permit. Do not treat this as a post-graduation plan. |
| **Talent visa discussion** | Fragomen reports a new talent-visa concept, but it should not be treated as a guaranteed route without current implementing rules and an eligible employer. | Verify before relying on it. |

### 3.2 Junior IT salary and hiring reality

| Indicator | 2026 figure | Read-through |
|---|---:|---|
| Junior specialist median | **RUB 88,000/month** ≈ USD 1,074 | Far below HQS threshold; about 35% of current monthly HQS equivalent. |
| Intern median | **RUB 64,000/month** | Internship is the realistic entry route. |
| Moscow junior range | **RUB 85,000–120,000/month** | Still below HQS. |
| Regional / remote junior range | **RUB 50,000–70,000/month** | Cheaper city does not help HQS eligibility. |
| Internships | **RUB 35,000–50,000/month** | Common stepping stone. |
| Direct junior hiring vs internship conversion | ~**25%** direct junior hire; ~**75%** via internship conversion | Plan for an internship-to-offer path. |
| Vacancies | IT vacancies fell from **66,149** to **48,380** | More competition for entry roles. |

**Realistic chance:** A Pakistani bachelor can legally work in Russia, but the combination of employer sponsorship, Russian-language hiring expectations, reduced junior vacancies, and the HQS salary gap makes immediate skilled migration difficult. The strongest route is: Russian language → accredited IT employer → internship → sponsored work visa/no-permit IT employment. HQS becomes relevant only later, when salary approaches **RUB 250,000/month** in 2026 or far higher in 2027.

### 3.3 Sanctions / geopolitics

- 2024 U.S. actions targeted Russian **IT services, software, design, support, cloud, and enterprise-management/manufacturing software** channels.
- A 2026 U.S. sanctions law adds broad **secondary sanctions**, tariffs up to **500%**, fund-transfer, securities, and financial-messaging restrictions, and anti-facilitation exposure.
- Direct application to a Pakistani national is **not automatic**; the greater practical effect is employer compliance, banking/payment delays, client restrictions, vendor availability, and reputational risk.
- Avoid assuming U.S.-linked clients, dollar invoicing, or Western software vendors will be usable. Ask the prospective employer’s legal/compliance team before accepting or paying relocation costs.

## 4. Malaysia

### 4.1 ALFA degree status

MQA lists **Alfa University College (AUC)**, previously **ALFA College**, and the **Bachelor of Information Technology (Honours)** with code **MQA/FA14725**. The listing shows accreditation starting **06/03/2026** exactly as displayed. Confirm that your award date/program cohort falls within the accredited period and keep the official degree, transcript, and MQA reference when applying for visas/EP.

### 4.2 Graduate Pass: available in principle, not to Pakistan under current list

The Graduate Pass is a **12-month, non-extendable** pass for Bachelor’s degree holders and above. Reported conditions include:

- valid **Student Pass** at the time of application;
- passport valid at least **18 months**;
- **12 months** local health insurance;
- sponsor/income evidence of **RM1,500+**;
- **part-time work only** in permitted sectors;
- EMGS marketing says “work without employer sponsor,” but the official guide/JIM FAQ describe part-time-only work, not unrestricted full-time employment.

**Eligible nationalities in the JIM FAQ (32):** United States, Saudi Arabia, Australia, Bahrain, Netherlands, Brunei, China, Denmark, Philippines, Finland, **India**, Indonesia, Germany, Japan, Canada, Cambodia, South Korea, Kuwait, Laos, Myanmar, New Zealand, Norway, Oman, France, Qatar, Singapore, Sweden, Switzerland, Thailand, UAE, United Kingdom, Vietnam.

**Pakistan is not listed.** On the published list, a Pakistani ALFA graduate is **not eligible for the Graduate Pass**. No Pakistan exception was found in the sources reviewed.

A further MIDA note narrows the Graduate Pass part-time work to campus employment, hospitality, petrol stations, mini markets, and restaurants, and says it is for postgraduates and above in Peninsular Malaysia. That conflicts with the broader bachelor-level wording elsewhere; verify with JIM/EMGS before planning any work under it.

### 4.3 Full-time work: employer-sponsored EP

| Route | Salary / rule | Fit for ALFA IT bachelor |
|---|---|---|
| **General Employment Pass (EP), effective 1 Jun 2026** | Cat I **RM20,000+**; Cat II **RM10,000–19,999**; Cat III **RM5,000–9,999**; manufacturing/MRS **RM7,000–9,999**. | Most junior IT offers at RM3,000–5,500 sit at or below the Cat III floor. An offer at **RM5,000+ basic** may qualify in principle; approval is employer/government discretionary. |
| **MIDA EP Foreign Graduate** | Five NIMP manufacturing sectors: **electronics & electrical, specialty chemicals, aerospace, pharmaceuticals, medical devices**. Requires **CGPA ≥3.50** or Master’s/PhD, **no experience required**, minimum **RM4,000/month**, **24 months**, no extension, employer-sponsored via **Xpats**, localisation plan, can begin while on a valid Student Pass or Graduate Social Visit Pass, and cannot convert to another pass after 24 months. | Potentially useful for a high-CGPA ALFA IT graduate only if hired into one of those five sectors. It is **not a generic IT graduate work pass**, and the lower RM4,000 floor should not be assumed for ordinary IT roles. |

### 4.4 Junior IT salary and hiring reality

| Indicator | 2026 figure | Read-through |
|---|---:|---|
| Junior developer range | **RM3,000–5,500/month** ≈ USD 729–1,336 | Overlaps the EP floor only at the top end. |
| Software engineer median | **RM5,000/month** ≈ USD 1,215 | Around the EP floor; basic salary composition matters. |
| 10th / 90th percentile | **RM2,400 / RM7,800** | Entry-level offers often below EP thresholds. |

**Realistic chance:** Malaysia is easier on language and sanctions risk, but the legal work gate is salary- and sponsor-driven. A Pakistani ALFA bachelor should not assume a Graduate Pass. The realistic full-time route is an employer willing to pay at least the applicable EP floor and file via Xpats, or the narrower MIDA graduate-EP route with high CGPA and a qualifying manufacturing-sector job.

## 6. Uncertainty / conflicts — do not ignore

1. **Graduate Pass marketing vs official rules:** EMGS marketing language suggests “work without employer sponsor,” but the EMGS guide/JIM FAQ say part-time only. MIDA’s note is narrower still and mentions postgraduates and specified sectors. Treat full-time work under Graduate Pass as unsupported.
2. **Pakistan exclusion:** The current JIM list includes India but not Pakistan. Treat Pakistani ineligibility as the working assumption unless JIM confirms otherwise in writing.
3. **RM4,000 vs RM5,000:** MIDA’s graduate EP floor is **RM4,000**, but the general EP Cat III floor from 1 Jun 2026 is **RM5,000**. These are different schemes; do not apply the lower figure to ordinary IT jobs.
4. **Russia language/exam conflict:** BBP and Forte differ on which quota/non-quota route requires the Russian-language/history/law certificate. Assume Russian is practically necessary and verify the exact permit category with the employer’s migration lawyer.
5. **HQS threshold timing:** 2026 threshold **RUB 750,000/quarter ≈ RUB 250,000/month**; reported 2027 threshold **RUB 717,000/month**. Gross-vs-net is not consistently clear in secondary sources.
6. **Salary data quality:** Salary figures are market estimates, not immigration approval guarantees. Basic salary, allowances, and “gross/net” definitions can change EP/HQS eligibility.
7. **ALFA accreditation date:** MQA displays **06/03/2026** as the accreditation start date. Verify your cohort/award date and keep MQA/degree documents.
